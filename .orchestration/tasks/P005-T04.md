## Required work

- Add a small pure `packages/server/src/lib/graphSubscriptionPolicy.ts` and tests.
- Canonical supported resource only: `/teams/{teamId}/channels/{channelId}/messages`; validate nonblank IDs.
- Max lifetime 4,320m. Use a documented safety buffer (5m) and renew margin comfortably above the 30m tick/outage (6h). One policy computes create/renew/lifecycle expiry.
